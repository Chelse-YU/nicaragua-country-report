# Nicaragua Macroeconomic Country Report

**University of Lausanne · HEC Lausanne**  
**Course:** International Money and Finance  
**Semester:** Spring 2024

## 1. Project Overview

This project examines Nicaragua's macroeconomic performance
and external position through official economic data.

The report focuses on economic growth, inflation, public
finances, balance of payments, international reserves, and
exchange-rate dynamics.

The objective is to assess Nicaragua's macroeconomic stability,
external vulnerabilities, and economic development potential.

## 2. Research Questions

- How has Nicaragua's macroeconomic performance evolved
  over the past two decades?
- What do its balance-of-payments components reveal about
  external dependence and financial resilience?
- How have nominal and real exchange rates evolved?
- What relationship can be observed between the inflation
  differential with the United States and the bilateral
  exchange rate?

## 3. Data Sources

Macroeconomic data are collected from approved official
sources, subject to the course's source requirements.

- Banco Central de Nicaragua (BCN)
- World Bank — World Development Indicators
- International Labour Organization (ILO)
- Bank for International Settlements (BIS), where available
- FRED, where appropriate

See [`sources/data_sources.md`](sources/data_sources.md)
for indicator definitions, source links, and data coverage.

## 4. Methodology

- Collect and organize historical macroeconomic data.
- Clean and harmonize time series using R.
- Calculate selected indicators as percentages of GDP.
- Visualize long-term trends and external-sector dynamics.
- Interpret the results using macroeconomic concepts.
